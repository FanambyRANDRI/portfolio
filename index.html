The through-line

Two tracks.
One purpose.

For more than four years at VoxCroft Analytics Inc., I have designed NLP, Machine Translation, and Speech Recognition systems for multilingual, time-sensitive work. Alongside that work, I conduct independent research on the behaviours and experiences that emerge when people share decisions, resources, and responsibility with AI.

I care about technology that is technically solid and useful to society, especially in African contexts, where language access, education, and human-centered services should never be an afterthought.

Evaluation of Hybrid Learning Approaches for Clay Mineral Detection

Pan-African Robotics Competition 2025 · 1st Place

Spectral soil data · Hybrid ML evaluation · SVM-led results

Impact: 1st Place winner with a robust SVM-led pipeline.

This research evaluates standalone and hybrid machine learning models for predicting clay content in soil using spectral data. The study found that a well-optimized SVM, combined with effective preprocessing and data augmentation, outperformed more complex hybrid architectures.
